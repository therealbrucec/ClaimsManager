New claim or existing claim?

This is the decision the whole desk turns on: is an inbound item a new loss, or more paper for a claim already open? Filing it against the wrong claim, or opening a second claim for the same loss, causes trouble for everyone downstream. Claims Manager searches for likely matches and shows you what it found, but you make the call.

The New claim or existing claim? window opens when you click Save and find claim while recording an item, or Find claim for this item in the intake queue.

How the search is filled in

The top of the window shows the item you are filing. Claims Manager reads its subject and text and fills in the search for you. A grey line under the search says what it found and where from.

  • A claim reference in the text, such as CLM-2026-00042, is as close to certain as this gets. The search uses that claim's policy number.
  • Otherwise, the first thing that looks like a policy number, such as NGC/884213, goes into Policy no.
  • The sender's surname goes into Insured.
  • The earliest date in the text, read day first (31/01/2026), goes into Date of loss.

These are hints only. Change any of them and click Search again.

How matches are found

The search is deliberately generous. A claim is listed if any of these is true:

  • its policy number contains what is in Policy no. The digits alone are also tried, so NGC884213 still finds NGC/884213;
  • its insured name contains what is in Insured;
  • with Date of loss ticked, its date of loss falls within the number of days either side given in +/- days (7 unless you change it).

It is better to be shown four possible claims and rule three out than to miss the right one and open a duplicate. Double-click a claim in the list to open it and check before you decide.

4 To file the item against an existing claim

  1. Select the claim in the list.
  2. Click Attach to selected claim, then Yes to confirm. Any files that came with the item are moved into that claim's folder.
  3. Click Yes if you want to open the claim now.

4 To open a new claim from the item

  1. Click Create new claim from this item. If there are possible matches in the list, Claims Manager asks you to confirm that this really is a new loss.
  2. A new claim opens, already filled in from the item: the sender as the insured, the received date as the date notified, the text as the description of what happened, the sender's email address or telephone number, and any policy number and date of loss found in the text. You are made the handler.
  3. Complete anything else you know and click Save claim. The claim is given its reference, the item is filed against it, and the claim opens.

Only handlers and supervisors can file items or open new claims.
