Northbridge Assurance Claims Manager

Roles and what they may do

Every account has one of three roles. The role, not the person, decides which commands are available and which moves a claim can make.

Handler
Owns the file from first contact to closure. Records post, decides whether it is a new claim or an existing one, chases documents, submits the claim to the underwriter and records payments.
Adjuster
Reviews the evidence and makes the technical call: approve, reject, or refer the claim back for more information. Reads the file rather than writing it, so the details of a claim are read-only to an adjuster.
Supervisor
Everything a handler can do, plus the only routes back: returning an approved claim to the adjuster, and reopening a claim that was rejected or declined.
