Northbridge Assurance Claims Manager

The main window

Everything in Claims Manager happens inside the main window. Claim lists, the intake queue and each claim you open appear as separate windows within it, so you can keep several claims open at once and move between them from the Window menu.

Menus

MenuCommandWhat it does
FileSign outCloses everything and returns to the sign-in box.
ExitCloses Claims Manager.
IntakeRecord inbound item…Records an email, fax, letter or call. See Recording an inbound item.

Open by reference…Opens a claim when you know its reference.
ReportsExport to Excel…Produces the management reports. See Exporting reports.
WindowClose all windowsCloses every open list and claim. The menu also lists the open windows; click one to bring it to the front.
HelpContentsOpens this help.
IndexOpens the help index.
AboutShows where the database, documents and sign-in file are kept.

Commands you are not permitted to use are greyed out. An adjuster, for example, cannot record post or open new claims.

Toolbar

The buttons under the menus are shortcuts to the commands used most: New claim, Record inbound item, Unmatched queue, My claims and Review queue.

Status bar

The bar along the bottom shows who is signed in, their role, and how many inbound items are waiting in the intake queue. The count updates whenever an item is recorded or filed.

Tip  Opening a claim that is already open brings its window to the front rather than opening it twice.
