Recording an inbound item

Claims Manager is not connected to the mailbox, the fax machine or the telephones. Everything that arrives about a claim is typed in by a handler as an inbound item: one email, fax, letter or call, together with any files that came with it. Everything else on the desk follows from getting this right.

4 To record an inbound item

  1. On the Intake menu, click Record inbound item, or press CTRL+I.
  2. In Arrived by, choose Email, Fax, Post or Phone.
  3. In Received, set the date and time it arrived. It starts at the current time; change it if you are catching up on yesterday's post.
  4. In From, type who sent it, and in Email or fax no, their address or number. You need at least one of the two.
  5. Type the Subject, and paste or type the Text of item. You need at least one of these as well.
  6. To add the files that came with it, choose a type in File this next as, click Add files, and select one or more files. Change the type before each batch: photographs, then the invoice, and so on.
  7. Click one of:
    • Save and find claim to save the item and go straight on to decide which claim it belongs to; or
    • Save to intake queue to save it and match it later. It waits in the intake queue.
Tip  Paste the whole text, including any reference numbers. When you come to match the item, Claims Manager reads claim references, policy numbers and dates out of the text and uses them to search for the claim.

4 To record an item against a claim you already have open

  1. On the claim, click the Correspondence tab.
  2. Click Record an item against this claim.
  3. Fill in the item as above, then click Save to claim. The item skips the intake queue and is filed against the claim at once.

About the attachments

  • Files are copied into the Claims Manager document store when you save, so the originals can be deleted afterwards.
  • To take a file off the list before saving, select it and click Remove selected.
  • An item filed against a claim has its files moved into that claim's own folder in the store.

If the item will not save

MessageWhat it means
Choose how this item arrived.Arrived by is empty.
Record who this came from…Both From and Email or fax no are empty.
Enter a subject or paste the text…Both Subject and Text of item are empty.
The received date is in the future.Correct Received.

Only handlers and supervisors can record inbound items.
