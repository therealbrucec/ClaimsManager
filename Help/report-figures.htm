Understanding the report figures

Both reports have one row for every month in the period, including months with nothing in them, so the chart line has no gaps. Claims are placed in a month by their date notified, not their date of loss: the reports answer "what came in when".

Claim value over time

ColumnWhat it shows
MonthThe month of notification.
Estimated valueThe total estimated value of the claims notified that month.
ReserveTheir total reserve.
Paid to dateThe total paid on them so far, less recoveries.
ClaimsHow many claims that is.

Only claims at the statuses ticked when the report was run are counted. The chart plots the three money columns; the claim count sits beside them, outside the chart, so it cannot flatten the money scale.

Claim frequency by month

ColumnWhat it shows
MonthThe calendar month.
Claims notifiedClaims notified that month.
Claims concludedClaims that reached Settled, Closed, Withdrawn, Adjuster Rejected or Underwriter Declined that month, taken from the claims' status history.
Open at month endA running total: the claims still open at the end of the month.

The running total starts from the number of claims already open when the period began, not from nought, so the first month is honest.

Note  A claim that concludes, is reopened by a supervisor and concludes again is counted in Claims concluded each time.
