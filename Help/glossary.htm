Northbridge Assurance Claims Manager

Glossary

Audit trail
The permanent record of what was done to a claim, by whom and when. Shown on the History tab.
Claim reference
The number Claims Manager gives a claim when it is first saved, such as CLM-2026-00042: the year it was opened and a running number. Quote it on every letter.
Concluded
A claim that has reached Settled, Closed, Withdrawn, Adjuster Rejected or Underwriter Declined. Used by the frequency report.
Date notified
The date Northbridge was first told about the loss.
Date of loss
The date the loss happened. Often earlier than the date notified, and sometimes not known at first.
Document store
The Documents folder beside the program, where every file attached to a claim is kept, one folder per claim reference.
Estimated value
The current best estimate of the total cost of a claim.
Handler
The member of staff who owns a claim from first contact to closure. See Roles.
Inbound item
One email, fax, letter or telephone call about a claim, recorded by a handler. See Recording an inbound item.
Insured
The policyholder making the claim.
Intake queue
Inbound items recorded but not yet filed against a claim. See The intake queue.
Interim payment
A payment on account, made before a claim is agreed in full.
Loss adjuster
A specialist who investigates a claim and recommends whether it should be paid. See Reviewing a claim as an adjuster.
Net paid
Everything paid on a claim, less anything recovered.
Peril
The cause of the loss, such as fire, flood or escape of water.
Policy number
The number of the policy the claim is made under, from the policy schedule.
Recovery
Money received back on a claim, for example from a third party responsible for the loss.
Reserve
The amount set aside to meet a claim. It must be set before a claim goes to the underwriter.
Settlement
Payment of a claim. A claim is marked Settled once the underwriter has agreed and payment has been recorded.
Status
Where a claim has got to. See Claim statuses.
Status history
The list of every change of status on a claim, with the reason given.
Supervisor
A handler who may also reverse decisions. See Roles.
Underwriter
The insurer, or the underwriter acting for it, who decides whether a claim is paid.
Withdrawn
A claim the insured no longer wishes to pursue. Withdrawal is final.
