Claim statuses

Every claim has a status, shown in the header of the claim window and in every claim list. The status says where the claim has got to and whose move it is next. There are twelve.

The usual path

Notified → (Documents Pending) → With Adjuster → Adjuster Approved → Sent To Underwriter → Underwriter Agreed → Settled → Closed

All statuses

StatusMeaningNext move by
NotifiedThe loss has been reported and a claim opened.Handler
Documents PendingWaiting for the insured to send something.Handler
With AdjusterA loss adjuster is reviewing the claim.Adjuster
Referred For InfoThe adjuster has asked the handler for more.Handler
Adjuster ApprovedThe adjuster supports the claim.Handler
Adjuster RejectedThe adjuster does not support the claim.Handler, to close it
Sent To UnderwriterSubmitted to the underwriter for a decision.Handler, when the reply comes
Underwriter AgreedThe underwriter will pay.Handler
Underwriter DeclinedThe underwriter will not pay.Handler, to close it
SettledPaid.Handler, to close it
ClosedFinished. No further moves.–
WithdrawnThe insured withdrew the claim. No further moves.–

Closed and Withdrawn are final. A supervisor can reopen a claim at Adjuster Rejected or Underwriter Declined by sending it back to the adjuster, but once a claim is closed or withdrawn it stays that way.

Changing status

A claim's status is changed only with Move claim on in the claim window. The moves offered depend on the current status and on your role, and some need a written reason. See:

  • Moving a claim on, for how;
  • Status changes reference, for every move and who may make it;
  • Before a claim can move on, for the checks made first.
