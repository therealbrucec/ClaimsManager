The intake queue

The intake queue holds every inbound item that has been recorded but not yet filed against a claim. Working the queue down to nothing is the first job of the day: until an item is matched, nobody looking at the claim can see it.

To open the queue, click Unmatched queue on the Intake menu or on the toolbar. The status bar at the bottom of the main window always shows how many items are waiting.

What you see

  • The list shows each waiting item, oldest first, so the item that has waited longest is at the top.
  • Selected item shows the full text of the item you have selected.
  • The list beneath it shows the files that came with the item. To look at one, select it and click Open attachment, or double-click it.

4 To file an item from the queue

  1. Select the item in the list.
  2. Click Find claim for this item, or double-click the item.
  3. Decide whether it belongs to an existing claim or a new one. See New claim or existing claim?

Once filed, the item leaves the queue and appears on the claim's Correspondence tab.

Other buttons

  • Record inbound item records something new straight into the queue.
  • Refresh reloads the list, to pick up items a colleague has just recorded.

Adjusters can see the queue but cannot record or file items.
