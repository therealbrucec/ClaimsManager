Reviewing a claim as an adjuster

A loss adjuster reviews the evidence on a claim and makes the technical call. Claims reach you when a handler refers them, and they wait in the review queue until you decide.

The review queue

Adjusters see the Adjuster review queue as soon as they sign in. It can also be opened from the Claims menu, or with Review queue on the toolbar.

  • Signed in as an adjuster, you see the claims With Adjuster that are assigned to you.
  • Signed in as a handler or supervisor, you see every claim With Adjuster, whoever it is assigned to.

The oldest notification is at the top. The search and status boxes are switched off, because the queue is defined by status.

4 To review a claim

  1. Double-click the claim in the queue.
  2. Read the Details, Correspondence and Documents tabs. Add any notes you need to.
  3. Click Move claim on and choose your decision:
    • Approve claim – the claim goes back to the handler to send to the underwriter;
    • Reject claim – the handler closes it;
    • Refer back for more information – the handler gets what you need and returns it to you.
  4. Type your reason and click Confirm. Every adjuster decision needs a reason, and it is recorded on the claim's history.

The claim leaves your queue as soon as you decide. If you referred it back, it returns when the handler clicks Return to adjuster.

Note  The Details tab is read-only for adjusters. If something in it is wrong, refer the claim back and say what in your reason.
