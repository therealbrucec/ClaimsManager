Northbridge Assurance Claims Manager

Details tab

The Details tab is the claim record itself: who is insured, what happened, what it is likely to cost, and who is dealing with it. Click Save claim after changing anything here.

Fields

FieldWhat to enter
Policy noThe policy the claim is made under, as it appears on the schedule.
InsuredThe policyholder's name. Required.
Postcode, Phone, EmailHow to reach the insured.
Date of lossWhen the loss happened. Tick Date is known to enter it; leave it unticked until you know. It cannot be in the future.
Date notifiedWhen Northbridge was first told about the loss.
PerilWhat caused the loss: Escape of Water, Fire, Storm, Flood, Theft, Malicious Damage, Accidental Damage, Impact, Subsidence, Public Liability, Employers Liability, Business Interruption or Other.
Estimated valueThe best current estimate of what the claim will cost.
ReserveThe amount set aside to pay the claim.
Paid netWhat has been paid, less recoveries. Worked out from the Payments tab; you cannot type here.
HandlerThe handler or supervisor responsible for the claim. The claim appears in their My claims.
Loss adjusterThe adjuster who will review the claim.
UnderwriterThe underwriter the claim will be submitted to.
What happenedA description of the loss in plain words.

Entering amounts

Type amounts as digits. 8400, 8,400.00 and £8400 are all accepted. A blank box counts as nothing.

What has to be filled in before the claim can move on

You can save a claim with only the insured's name, but it cannot go to an adjuster until the date of loss, peril, estimated value and loss adjuster are filled in, and cannot go to the underwriter without an underwriter and a reserve. See Before a claim can move on.

Note  Adjusters see this tab read-only. If something in it is wrong, refer the claim back to the handler with a note saying what.
