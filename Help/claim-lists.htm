Northbridge Assurance Claims Manager

Finding a claim

There are three lists of claims, all on the Claims menu:

  • My claims shows the claims on which you are the handler.
  • All claims shows every claim in the book.
  • Adjuster review queue shows claims waiting for an adjuster's decision. See Reviewing a claim as an adjuster.

4 To search a list

  1. In Find, type all or part of a claim reference, a policy number or the insured's name.
  2. Optionally, choose a Status to see only claims at that stage.
  3. Press ENTER or click Search.

Click Clear to empty the search and show the list as it started.

4 To open a claim from a list

  • Double-click the claim; or
  • select it and press ENTER, or click Open.

4 To open a claim by its reference

  1. On the Claims menu, click Open by reference.
  2. Type the reference, for example CLM-2026-00001, and click OK.

Open claims only

With Open claims only ticked, a list shows only the claims that still need somebody to do something: Notified, Documents Pending, With Adjuster, Referred For Info, Adjuster Approved, Sent To Underwriter and Underwriter Agreed.

Most of a year-old book is settled or closed, so the line under the list tells you how many claims the tick is holding back. Untick it to see them.

Important  Claims at Adjuster Rejected or Underwriter Declined do not count as open, but they still need closing. Untick Open claims only, or choose the status, to find them.

Reading the list

The Status column is coloured so a full list can be read at a glance:

ColourStatusesMeaning
NavyWith Adjuster, Sent To UnderwriterWaiting on someone outside the handler's desk.
GreenAdjuster Approved, Underwriter Agreed, SettledA positive decision has been made.

GreyClosedFinished.
