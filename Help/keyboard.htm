Keyboard shortcuts

The underlined letter in a menu, button or field label is its access key: hold down ALT and press the letter. If the underlines are not showing, press ALT once to reveal them. TAB moves from one field to the next.

Anywhere

PressTo
F1Open help on the window, or the claim tab, you are using.
CTRL+NOpen a new claim.
CTRL+IRecord an inbound item.
CTRL+F6Move to the next window inside Claims Manager.
CTRL+F4Close the window you are in.
ALT+F4Exit Claims Manager.

In a claim list

PressTo
ENTER in FindSearch.
ENTER in the listOpen the selected claim.
UP ARROW, DOWN ARROWMove through the list.

In the sign-in box

PressTo
ENTERSign in.
