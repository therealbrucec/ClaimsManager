Before a claim can move on

Three moves check that the claim is ready before they are allowed. If something is missing, Claims Manager says what, and the claim stays where it is.

To With Adjuster

Applies to Refer to loss adjuster, Return to adjuster, Send back to adjuster and Reopen with adjuster. On the Details tab the claim needs:

  • a date of loss;
  • a peril;
  • an estimated value above nothing;
  • a loss adjuster.

It also needs at least one document or one inbound item. An adjuster needs something to review.

To Sent To Underwriter

Applies to Send to underwriter. On the Details tab the claim needs:

  • an underwriter;
  • a reserve above nothing.

To Settled

Applies to Record settlement. At least one payment must be recorded on the Payments tab, so that the net paid is more than nothing.
