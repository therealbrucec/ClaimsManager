Northbridge Assurance Claims Manager

The claim window

Each claim opens in its own window, titled with its reference and the insured's name. The window holds the whole claim file.

The header

Across the top of the window are the claim reference and its current status, and three buttons:

ButtonWhat it does
Move claim on…The only way to change the claim's status. It lists the moves your role can make from the current status. If there are none, the button reads No action available. See Moving a claim on.
Save claimSaves the Details tab.
CloseCloses the window. Unsaved changes on the Details tab are lost.

The tabs

TabWhat it holds
DetailsThe insured, the loss, the money and who is dealing with it.
CorrespondenceEvery email, fax, letter and call filed against the claim.
DocumentsPhotographs, estimates, invoices, reports and other files.
NotesFile notes.
PaymentsMoney paid out and recovered.
HistoryEvery change of status, with the reason, and the audit trail.

Press F1 on any tab for help on that tab.

A new claim

A new claim reads New claim and Not saved yet in the header. Only the Details tab is available until you save it, because nothing can be attached to a claim that has no reference. The first save gives the claim its reference, in the form CLM-2026-00001, and its status of Notified.

If you are an adjuster

The Details tab is read-only, and you cannot add documents, record items or payments. You can add notes, and the Move claim on button offers your decisions when the claim is with you.
