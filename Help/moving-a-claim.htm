Moving a claim on

Referring a claim to an adjuster, approving it, sending it to the underwriter, settling it, closing it: every step is a change of status, and every change of status is made the same way.

4 To move a claim on

  1. Open the claim.
  2. Click Move claim on in the header. A menu lists the moves you can make from the claim's current status.
  3. Click the move you want.
  4. If the claim is not ready for that move, a message says what is missing. Put it right, then start again. See Before a claim can move on.
  5. The Confirm decision box shows the change about to be made, from one status to the other. Type the reason in Reason, recorded on the claim file.
  6. Click Confirm.

The new status appears in the header at once, and a line is added to the History tab with your name and the reason.

Reasons

Some moves cannot be made without a reason: requesting documents, withdrawing a claim, every adjuster decision, recording an underwriter's decline, closing a rejected claim, and every supervisor override. The box title reads Confirm decision - reason required and Confirm will not go ahead with the reason empty.

For other moves a reason is optional, but it helps whoever picks the claim up next.

Note  Changes you have typed on the Details tab are saved before the move is made, provided they are valid. If they are not (a date of loss in the future, say), they are not saved, and the tab goes back to the saved details once the move is made. Save the claim first if in doubt.

If the button says No action available

Nothing can be done to the claim at its current status by someone in your role. For example, an adjuster has nothing to do with a claim at Notified, and nobody can move a claim that is Closed or Withdrawn.
