Correspondence tab

The Correspondence tab lists every inbound item filed against the claim: each email, fax, letter and telephone call, newest at the top.

  • Select an item to read it in full in the box beside the list. The box shows how it arrived, when, who from and the subject, then the text.
  • Came with this item lists the files that arrived with the selected item. To look at one, select it and click Open attachment, or double-click it.

4 To record a new item straight onto this claim

  1. Click Record an item against this claim.
  2. Fill in the item. See Recording an inbound item.
  3. Click Save to claim.

Use this when you already know which claim the item belongs to, for example a reply to a letter you sent. Anything else should go through the intake queue, so that the duplicate check is made.

Note  Files that arrive with an item also appear on the Documents tab.
