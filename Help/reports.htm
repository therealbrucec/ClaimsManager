Northbridge Assurance Claims Manager

Exporting reports

Claims Manager produces two management reports as Microsoft Excel workbooks. Each is a worksheet of monthly figures with a line chart drawn underneath them.

  • Claim value over time – estimated value, reserve and amount paid for the claims notified each month.
  • Claim frequency by month – how many claims arrived each month, how many concluded, and how many were left open at the month end.

What the columns mean is explained in Understanding the report figures.

4 To export a report

  1. On the Reports menu, click Export to Excel.
  2. Under Period, set From and To. Last 12 months puts back the default of the year to today.
  3. For the value report, tick the statuses to include under Claim status. Select all and Clear all tick and untick everything; Open claims ticks only the open statuses.
  4. Click Export claim value over time or Export claim frequency by month.
  5. Choose where to save the workbook and click Save. It is named after the report and the period, in your Documents folder unless you choose otherwise.
  6. Wait while Excel builds the workbook, then click Yes to open it.
Tip  Tick only Settled to chart what the book actually cost, or click Open claims to chart what is still exposed.
Note  The status ticks affect only the value report. The frequency report always counts every claim.

Without Excel

The charts are drawn by Excel itself, so Excel must be installed on the computer. If it cannot be started, Claims Manager offers to save the figures as a CSV file instead, without a chart. You can also choose CSV file in the Save as type box to get the figures alone.
