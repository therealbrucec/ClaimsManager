Northbridge Assurance Claims Manager

Welcome to Claims Manager

Claims Manager is the claims handling desk for Northbridge Assurance. It records everything that arrives about a loss, whether by email, fax, post or telephone, helps you decide whether it belongs to a claim already open or to a new one, and carries each claim from first notification, through review by a loss adjuster and a decision by the underwriter, to payment and closure.

How a claim moves through the desk

  1. Post arrives. A handler records each email, fax, letter or call as an inbound item, together with any files that came with it.
  2. New or existing? Each item waits in the intake queue until a handler files it against a claim or opens a new claim from it.
  3. The file is built. The handler completes the claim details, gathers documents and refers the claim to a loss adjuster.
  4. The adjuster decides. The adjuster approves, rejects or refers the claim back for more information.
  5. The underwriter decides. An approved claim is sent to the underwriter, who agrees to pay or declines.
  6. Payment and closure. Payments are recorded, the claim is marked settled and, finally, closed.

Each of those steps is a change of status. Claims Manager only offers the moves that are allowed from the claim's current status, for your role, and it checks the claim is ready before it lets it move on.

Getting help

  • Press F1 in any window for help on that window. On a claim, F1 opens the help for whichever tab you are looking at.
  • On the Help menu, click Contents to browse the help, or Index to look up a word.
  • New to the terms? See the Glossary.
Note  This is a demonstration system. The policyholders, claims and figures in it are invented, and every account shares one password.
