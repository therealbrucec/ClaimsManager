Payments tab

The Payments tab records money paid out on the claim, and money recovered from someone else.

4 To record a payment

  1. Under Record a payment, choose the Type:
    • Interim – a payment on account before the claim is agreed in full;
    • Final – the payment that settles the claim;
    • Fees – a professional fee, such as the loss adjuster's;
    • Recovery – money received back, for example from a third party's insurer.
  2. Type the Amount. It must be more than nothing. Enter a recovery as a positive amount too; its type is what makes it count against the total.
  3. Type who the Payee is. This is required.
  4. Optionally, type a Reference, such as the cheque or BACS reference.
  5. Set Paid on to the date of payment.
  6. Click Record payment.

The totals

The line under the list shows:

  • Net paid – every payment that is not a recovery, less every recovery. This is the figure shown as Paid net on the Details tab.
  • Reserve – the reserve from the Details tab.
  • Outstanding – the reserve less the net paid.
Note  A claim cannot be marked Settled until at least one payment has been recorded. When it is settled, the net paid is recorded as the settled amount.

Payments cannot be edited or deleted. To reverse a payment made in error, record a recovery for the same amount and add a note explaining why.

Adjusters can see payments but cannot record them.
